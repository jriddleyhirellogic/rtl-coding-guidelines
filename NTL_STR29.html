NTL_STR29

Message: Pulse generator

Description This rule fires when Leda detects a pulse generator in the design. A net that is gated with its inverted counterpart net by two input NAND, AND, NOR, OR, XNOR, or XOR gates can produce a pulse generator. Removing pulse generators from the design helps stabilize the circuit.
Policy DESIGN
Ruleset STRUCTURE
Language VHDL/Verilog
Type Hardware
Severity Error

Example

This is an example of invalid Verilog code, followed by a circuit diagram that illustrates the problem: 

module ntl_str29_top;
...
wire net_01, net_inv, net_02;
not   I1(net_inv, net_01);
and  I2(net_02, net_inv, net_01); 
// NTL_STR29 fires -- pulse generator
endmodule
