NTL_CLK47

Message: Clock of flip-flop/latch is unconstrained

Description This rule fires when leda finds flops or latches whose clocks are unconstrained. A clock is taken as unconstrained under the following condition: It is not defined in constraints file.
 It can not be propagated to the flop or latch because of black box.
 It cannot be propagated to the flop or latch because of gates in the path whose definitions are not available in DB file.
Policy DESIGN
Ruleset CLOCKS
Language Verilog/VHDL
Type Hardware
Severity Error
